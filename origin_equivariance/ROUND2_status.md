## What the referee accepted / what was fixed after round 1

- Statistics: unit-level inference is now a permutation test (p = 0.009, n = 11);
  cell-level correlations are labelled descriptive everywhere.
- The classifier result is replicated on a second exposed dataset
  (BCI-2a, classes 1/2: Spearman -0.654, p = 0.015; BCI-2b: -0.78).
- Composition with second-order alignment added: the mean correction still
  gains +0.0725 / +0.0236 inside the Euclidean-aligned representation, and
  +0.0417 / +0.0452 inside the tangent representation.
- Abstract reframed around risk (the benefit is not screenable; stated as a
  negative result). Abstract cut to 250 words. Figures remade, captions added,
  references cleaned (17 cited = 17 listed).

## What is still open (the referee's remaining majors)

1. **Prevalence is still unmeasured.** The single highest-value addition: a
   small audit (10-15 public pipelines: MOABB, Braindecode, pyRiemann, MNE
   examples, BCI-IV baselines) quoting the exact lines that centre one side.
   Without it the paper reads as a straw man at BSPC.
2. **The benefit screen.** z_T does not order the gain (p = 0.57). Options:
   (i) find a statistic that does (the per-session offset displacement t has a
   weak cell-level signal, +0.25); (ii) keep the risk-only framing.
3. **Streaming experiment is a simulation with a random trial order.** Either
   run it with the real trial order and a convergence criterion, or cut it.
4. **Novelty framing.** The paper still needs one paragraph that states what a
   reader can do after it that they could not do before, in method terms
   (screen + certificate + unit test as a protocol), stated without hedging.
5. Soft: the residual subsection is thin; the calibration claim should be
   limited to BCI-2b or given per-subject CIs.

## Recommended order for round 3

1. Prevalence audit (web), 10-15 pipelines, quotes in a table.
2. Streaming with real trial order + convergence criterion (cheap).
3. Rewrite Sections 1, 5 and 7 around the protocol, then re-review.
4. If the audit finds few occurrences, reframe as a reproducibility/methods
   paper and say so explicitly in the first paragraph.
