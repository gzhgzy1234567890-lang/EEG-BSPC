[[59]] Which tests are primary. Five tests were pre-specified as one family with Holm control: the subject-level Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison of mean against none, per dataset. After Holm the p-values are 3.05e-4 (SEED), 0.016 (BCI-2a), 0.016 (BCI-2b), 0.104 (grasp-and-lift) and 0.320 (NER2015), so the gain survives correction on three datasets, not five. The family is pre-specified, but it is not five independent confirmations of one causal claim, and this paper does not present it that way: SEED is estimated on the released DE features whose preprocessing we did not control, BCI-2a and BCI-2b were already used during development, and the two remaining datasets fail after correction. Section 4.8 analyses one BCI endpoint under a fixed protocol with a causal filter and a source-only alignment, and that endpoint is reported separately as prospective-in-explored-data rather than folded into a single confirmed chain. Everything outside the five-test family is exploratory without a multiplicity correction, and per-dataset numbers are reported with their intervals throughout.

[[60]] Preprocessing detail. The 2a/2b features use a fourth-order Butterworth 8-30 Hz band-pass on the continuous signal. BCI-2a is epoched from 2.0 to 6.0 s after the trial onset, which is 0.0 to 4.0 s after the cue (the cue follows the trial marker by 2 s in that dataset, per the official data-set description [26]); BCI-2b is epoched from 0.5 to 3.5 s after the cue, which is 3.5 to 6.5 s after the trial marker (there the cue follows the marker by 3 s, measured from the GDF annotations). The two windows are therefore not the same, and this is a protocol difference between the datasets rather than a common setting; euc is a per-trial log-variance in four consecutive time windows of the epoch; csp takes the generalised eigenvectors of the first class covariance against the average covariance, three filters per class, fitted on the training session; tan maps the session covariance to the tangent space; euc_ea divides by the session covariance. LDA uses solver=lsqr and shrinkage=auto.
