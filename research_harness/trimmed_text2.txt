[[95]] The comparisons above are observational: they say which estimator wins where, not why. A controlled generator - constant offset, linear drift, step changes at block boundaries, or class-proportion change - reproduces the qualitative pattern with no uniformly superior rule. A constant offset is repaired by every rule (0.500 to about 0.70). Under drift all rules improve on no correction, and the adaptive window gains most (0.519 to 0.663 against 0.617 for the cumulative mean and 0.602 for the variance-ratio rule). Under step changes the ordering is the same and the spread is larger (0.342 to 0.607 for ADWIN, 0.440 for the cumulative mean). Under class-prior change every corrected rule sits slightly below no correction (0.704 against 0.688-0.698). The simulations are hypotheses about mechanisms, not causes, and they do not identify which mechanism dominates a recording.
